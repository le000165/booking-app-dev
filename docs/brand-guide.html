09 — Motion
Animation Principles
Motion should feel instant and purposeful. Nothing should animate longer than 300ms in routine interactions. Avoid bounces, spring overloads, or decorative animations that slow users down.

11 — Usage Guidelines
Do's and Don'ts
Brand integrity is maintained through consistent decisions. When in doubt, default to restraint — fewer colors, less motion, more whitespace.
✓ Do
  • Use Deep Navy as the primary brand color in all contexts
  • Use Cyan only for CTAs, focus rings, and active highlights
  • Maintain Inter as the exclusive UI typeface
  • Keep the minimum clear space around the logo at all times
  • Use the mark on navy container, never on colored or image backgrounds directly
  • Scale the wordmark proportionally — never stretch or compress
  • Use border-only cards with subtle shadows; avoid heavy insets
  • Prefer flat design — a single cyan accent line over heavy gradients
  • Use tight letter-spacing (−0.3 to −1.5px) for headings
  • Show status via semantic badges, not raw colored text
✗ Don't
  • Don't place the logo on busy images, patterns, or low-contrast surfaces
  • Don't use gradients as backgrounds for primary UI surfaces
  • Don't use the cyan color for body text or informational content
  • Don't round buttons or cards beyond 12px in dashboard contexts
  • Don't use more than 4 colors in a single component
  • Don't add drop shadows to the logo mark
  • Don't use the monochrome mark in color contexts — use the full brand version
  • Don't animate interactions longer than 300ms in routine flows
  • Don't use wave, sun, or ocean iconography — no tropical theming
  • Don't use emoji, rounded cartoon icons, or web3-style visual elements
Brand Voice — Copy Direction
Tone
Direct, confident, calm. No exclamation points in product UI. No filler phrases like "Easily manage…" — just describe what it does.
CTAs
Action-first, specific. "Book appointment" not "Get started". "View calendar" not "Explore". Avoid vague marketing verbs in product context.
Empty states
"No bookings for today" not "Looks like you're all clear! 🎉". Professional, informative, actionable. Always include a clear next action.
